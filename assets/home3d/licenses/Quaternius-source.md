# Quaternius home furniture

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatehomeinterior.html

Author: Quaternius. The official home interior page identifies its models as CC0 and permits personal and commercial projects. License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Modification is allowed; attribution is not required. BYND voluntarily records the author.

The original FBX files are from the user's downloaded `FBX-20260926T073204Z-1-001/FBX` subset, supplied as the Quaternius home assets. That subset does not include its own license document; this source record preserves the user-provided attribution and the official pack's CC0 statement rather than inventing an attached license. Exact original filenames, processing and exported checksums are in `../asset-manifest.json`.

BYND converts these geometry bases to self-contained GLB, merges meshes by original material, applies its own material system, and adds rounded upholstery. No other online models are used.
